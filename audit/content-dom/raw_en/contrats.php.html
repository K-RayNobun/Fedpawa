Our Contractual Solutions and Security Mechanisms

📝 1. Commercial, Service & Business Partnership Contracts

We secure your relationships with your commercial ecosystem (customers, suppliers, service providers):
  • Drafting of service agreements, subcontracting and joint-contracting contracts.
  • Conception de baux professionnels et commerciaux conformes aux exigences strictes de l'OHADA.
  • Drafting of distribution, franchise, commercial agency and supply agreements.
  • Preparation of General Terms and Conditions of Sale (GTC) and General Terms of Use (GTU) for your physical or e-commerce platforms.

👥 2. Employment Contracts & Corporate Agreements (HR Management)

We protect your company against the risk of labour litigation (Labour Court):
  • Drafting of tailored employment contracts (permanent, fixed-term, apprenticeship, internship) in full alignment with the Cameroonian Labour Code and applicable collective agreements.
  • Inclusion of specific protective clauses: non-compete clauses, strict confidentiality clauses (NDA), training repayment or exclusivity clauses.
  • Assistance in drafting company internal regulations, social charters and contractual amendments.

🔒 3. Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) & Specific Security

Protect your trade secrets before any partnership:
  • Drafting of unilateral or mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDA) to protect your trade secrets, concepts and negotiations before any partnership.

🔍 4. Contract Audit & Risk Management

Before you sign a document proposed by a third party (bank, multinational, State), we go through the instrument with a fine-tooth comb:
  • Identification of hidden, one-sided or disproportionate clauses that unbalance the agreement.
  • Assessment of civil liability mechanisms, penalty clauses (late-payment penalties) and termination conditions.
  • Drafting of legal counter-proposals to rebalance the negotiation in your favour.

🔐 5. Document Security & Private Cloud Archiving

We protect your major instruments against loss, falsification or theft:
  • Verification of the validity of signatures and the signatories' chain of authority.
  • High-definition digitisation and methodical indexing of your legal archives.
  • A secure Digital Safe (private FEDPAWA cloud) so you can access your strategic contracts 24/7, anywhere in the world, fully encrypted.
