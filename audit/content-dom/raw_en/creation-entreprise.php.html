Our Methodology

The Complete 4-Step Registration Process

Complete end-to-end handling, with no travel required on your part.

01

🔍 Step 1: Preliminary Audit & Strategic Orientation

Before launching any procedure, we analyze your project to validate the fundamental choices:

  • Choice of Legal Form: orientation objective entre l'Sole Proprietorship (Nom propre), la SARL (Société à Responsabilité Limitée), la SAS (Société par Actions Simplifiée) ou la SA (Public Limited Company) selon vos ambitions et le nombre d'associés.
  • Structuration du Capital Social : advice on share allocation, cash or in-kind contributions, and the protection of founding partners.
  • Validation de la Gouvernance : definition of the powers of the manager or president, and of decision-making rules (blocking clauses, required majority).
02

✍️ Step 2: Design & Drafting of the Legal Instruments

We do not use generic templates. Your documents are drafted on a tailor-made basis by business law experts:

  • Rédaction des Statuts de la société en stricte conformité avec les Actes Uniformes de l'OHADA.
  • Drafting of the Subscription and Payment Declaration (DSV).
  • Drafting of the minutes appointing the first officers (Managers, Presidents, Managing Directors).
03

🏛️ Step 3: The Administrative Circuit & Registration

We handle the entire institutional journey. You have no travel to perform:

  • Interface Notariale : case follow-up with our partner notary offices in Douala and Yaoundé for the execution and certification of the deeds.
  • Filing with the Registry & CFCE / Tax Registration: handling of the formalities and obtainment of the official registers.
04

💼 Step 4: Delivery of the "Corporate Kit"

At the end of the procedure, we hand over your complete legal file, organized and secured, including:

  • The original copy of the notarised articles.
  • Votre titre de Registre du Commerce (RCCM).
  • Votre Numéro d'identifiant unique (NIU) et votre Carte de Contribuable active.

NB: this complete kit is immediately usable to open your professional bank accounts in Cameroon and sign your first contracts.

Structuration

Overview of the Main Legal Forms

We objectively steer you towards the structure best suited to your vision.

Sole Proprietorship

Ideal for traders and self-employed starters, with no minimum capital requirement.

SARL / SARLU

The most widespread structure, protecting personal assets with structured management for one or more partners.

SAS / SASU

Prized by startups and investors for its great contractual and governance flexibility.

SA (Public Limited Company)

Reserved for large investment projects requiring a structured board of directors.
