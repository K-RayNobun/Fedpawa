🛑 Contractual Framework & Legal Security

To guarantee total flexibility for our clients, all of our hosting solutions are governed exclusively by an Infrastructure and Services Provision Agreement.

This legal act expressly excludes the status of commercial leases within the meaning of the Uniform OHADA Law. Consequently, this agreement grants no right to renewal, no commercial property and no right to remain in the premises for the subscriber. It is a provision of material, logistical and technological services, entirely flexible and revocable, ensuring perfect legal security and total freedom for both parties.
