Tax Outsourcing: A Major Strategic Investment

Face à la complexification du Code Général des Impôts et à la digitalisation stricte des procédures sur the portal of the Directorate General of Taxation (DGI), corporate tax management leaves no room for improvisation.

Outsourcing your tax monitoring to business-law experts avoids costly mistakes, protects your cash flow and builds an impeccable reputation with institutions.

⚠️ Protect your company from tax blocking!

A late filing or a missed instalment can instantly block your taxpayer card, make it impossible to obtain your Tax Compliance Certificate (ACF) and lead to the rejection of your public procurement bids. FEDPAWA watches over your deadlines month after month.

Our Fields of Intervention and Comprehensive Services

Our Fields of Intervention and Comprehensive Services

📊 1. Management of Current Monthly Declarations

We handle all mandatory periodic obligations on the official DGI e-filing portal:
  • Calculation, verification and e-filing of Value Added Tax (VAT).
  • Declaration and settlement of corporate or income tax instalments.
  • Management of withholding taxes on rents and local or international service providers.
  • Payroll tax filings (personal income tax, CNPS social contributions, Crédit Foncier, FNE).

📑 2. Preparation of the Annual Tax Return (DSF)

At the end of the financial year, we manage the major legal and tax transition:
  • Vérification de la conformité des écritures au regard du système comptable OHADA (SYSCOHADA).
  • Preparation, legal validation and filing of your Statistical and Tax Declaration (DSF).
  • Optimisation of legal tax deductions, depreciation and loss carry-forwards allowed by the General Tax Code.

🏛️ 3. Administrative Representation & Tax Compliance

We manage your day-to-day relations with your tax office (CIME, CDI or DGE):
  • Suivi de la délivrance et du maintien de votre Attestation de Conformité Fiscale (ACF), indispensable pour l'ensemble de vos démarches administratives, commerciales et bancaires.
  • Follow-up of VAT credit refund requests or tax compensation.
  • Permanent updating of your tax file (change of director, capital changes, etc.).
